How this is built

Every row is the generate_lead event from GA4, grouped by the "Nearest City" field selected on the contact and consultation forms. Paid / Organic / Other come from GA4's own channel grouping, not from a hand-built list — the same classification GA4 uses everywhere else.

Paid — Paid Search, Paid Social, Paid Shopping, Display, Cross-network, and similar Organic — Organic Search, Organic Social, Organic Video, Organic Shopping Other — Direct, Referral, Email, Unassigned, and anything else GA4 couldn't place in the first two

This visit vs. Ever acquired: the first uses the channel of the specific visit the lead happened in; the second uses however this browser was first ever acquired, even if that was months earlier. They can legitimately disagree — someone can be organically acquired long ago and still click a paid ad on the visit that finally converts.

"(not set)" means the lead came through without a city selected on the form — worth a look if that count is climbing.
