How this is built

Every row is the generate_lead event from GA4, grouped by the "Nearest City" field selected on the contact and consultation forms. Direct / Organic / Paid / Other come from GA4's own channel grouping, not from a hand-built list — the same classification GA4 uses everywhere else.

Direct — no referrer at all; typed the URL, used a bookmark, or arrived from an untracked link Organic — Organic Search, Organic Social, Organic Video, Organic Shopping Paid — Paid Search, Paid Social, Paid Shopping, Display, Cross-network, Affiliates Other — Referral, Email, SMS, Unassigned, and anything else GA4 couldn't place

This visit vs. Ever acquired: the first uses the channel of the specific visit the lead happened in; the second uses however this browser was first ever acquired, even if that was months earlier. They can legitimately disagree — someone can be organically acquired long ago and still click a paid ad on the visit that finally converts.

generate_lead vs form_submit: these two fire on the same submission, so the counts should track each other closely. A form_submit with no matching lead usually means the lead tag didn't fire — a broken selector, a validation bounce, or a form that isn't tagged yet. Both events are recent additions to the property, so treat any period that starts before they were installed as partial.

Direct is not the same as Unassigned. Direct means GA4 saw a real session that arrived with no referrer. Unassigned means GA4 couldn't tie the event to any acquisition at all — normally a broken link between the server-side lead and the browsing session it belongs to. Direct now has its own column so the two stop looking alike.

Why the pages table can look lopsided: generate_lead is sent server-side, so unless the payload includes page_location, GA4 has no page to file it under and it lands in "(not set)". form_submit fires in the browser and will show the real path. All the forms on one page and all the leads under "(not set)" is that gap, not a reporting fault.

"(not set)" means the lead came through without a city selected on the form — worth a look if that count is climbing. On campaign rows it means the visit carried no campaign parameter.
